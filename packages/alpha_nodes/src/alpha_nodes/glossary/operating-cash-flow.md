## Definition

Cash received from customers less cash paid for operations, before investment and financing.

## How we calculate it

An ingested line item of the annual fundamentals frame, in the reporting currency's major unit: provider-neutral, nullable (a missing item is null, never 0), taken from the filing known on the as-of day.

## Why it matters

It is an input to the fundamentals calcs listed under related terms.

## Shortcomings and pitfalls

Working-capital timing can swing it from year to year.

## Similar terms

See [free cash flow](fcf.md), [capital expenditure](capex.md).

## Prompt note

Net cash from operating activities for the annual period. Null when unreported.
